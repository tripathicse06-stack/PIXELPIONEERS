About the event

One weekend. One idea. Built brick by brick.

BrickByte 2026 is a 24-hour hybrid hackathon run by a GDG-inspired student community. Form a team, pick a real-world problem, build a working prototype, and demo it to mentors and judges.

Great products are never built in one go. They are built one small, solid brick at a time.

— The BrickByte idea

Hosted at

Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology

BrickByte is built by the GDG-inspired community of MMMUT, Gorakhpur: students who learn, build and ship together, and now invite the whole region to do the same.

Why we run it

Our goals

  • Turn classroom concepts into working products
  • Solve problems relevant to Purvanchal and India
  • Grow a community of builders at MMMUT

Who it's for

Who can join

  • Students from any college or branch
  • Beginners are welcome, no prior hackathon needed
  • Teams of 1 to 4 members

What's in it for you

What you get

  • Mentor sessions with industry and campus experts
  • Meals, swag and certificates for all participants
  • Prizes, goodies and a chance to showcase your work

  • Freeentry for every team

Schedule

Two days, woven into one timeline

Your 24-hour roadmap, from check-in to prizes. All times are in Indian Standard Time (IST).

  1. Day 109:00 – 10:00 AM

    Registration & Check-in

    Collect your badge, meet your team and settle in at your workstation.

  2. Day 110:00 – 10:45 AM

    Opening Ceremony & Rules

    Welcome note from GDG MMMUT, track briefing and judging criteria.

  3. Day 111:00 AM

    Hacking Begins

    The 24-hour build starts. Time to put your first brick down.

  4. Day 104:00 – 06:00 PM

    Mentor Round

    Get feedback on your idea and approach from mentors.

  5. Day 108:00 PM

    Dinner & Brick Challenge

    Refuel, then try a fun 20-minute LEGO-style build-off between teams.

  6. Day 1 · Night12:00 AM

    Midnight Snacks & Stand-up

    Grab a bite, share your progress in 60 seconds and cheer on other teams.

  7. Day 1 · Night02:00 AM

    Night Owl Mentor Desk

    Stuck on a bug? Mentors stay on call for quick help through the night.

  8. Day 208:00 AM

    Breakfast & Final Sprint

    Refuel, polish your demo and prepare your 5-minute pitch.

  9. Day 211:00 AM

    Code Freeze & Submissions Close

    Stop coding and submit your repository, demo link and short pitch.

  10. Day 212:00 – 02:00 PM

    Demos & Judging

    Each team gets 5 minutes to demo, followed by judge questions.

  11. Day 204:00 – 05:00 PM

    Closing & Prize Ceremony

    Winners announced, certificates handed out, and group photo.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need prior hackathon experience?

No. Beginners are welcome, and mentors will be available throughout to help you get unstuck.

How big can a team be?

Teams can have 1 to 4 members. You can also join solo and find teammates at check-in.

Is it free to participate?

Yes. Registration is free, and meals are provided to offline participants.

Can I join online?

Yes. BrickByte is hybrid, so you can build from anywhere and demo over a video call.

When does registration close?

Registration closes on 5 December 2026. The event begins on 12 December 2026 at 9:00 AM IST.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, charger, student ID and a generous measure of curiosity. We will provide the Wi-Fi, refreshments and plenty of inspiration.
